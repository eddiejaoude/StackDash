## What you can do without asking

- Say that your project works with, connects to, or is a fork of StackDash, in plain text, as long

- Run an unmodified copy of StackDash for yourself or your own organization, and call it
  StackDash.
- Write about StackDash, link to it, and use the name in articles, talks, and tutorials.

## What needs a different name

- A modified version you distribute or run for other people: give it its own name and logo. Keep
  the license and copyright notices as the AGPL requires.
- A hosted service that offers StackDash, modified or not, to people outside your organization.
- Product names, domains, and social accounts that include "StackDash", or a name or logo close
  enough to be mistaken for it.
