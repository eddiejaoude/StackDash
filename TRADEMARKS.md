page says how you can use them.

## What you can do without asking

- Run StackDash for yourself or your own organization, modified or not, with its name and logo.
- Share unmodified copies of StackDash under its name and logo, as the AGPL allows.
- Fork the repository to contribute changes back, and keep the repository name while you do.
- Say that your project works with, connects to, or is a fork of StackDash, in plain text, as long
  as nobody could read it as official or endorsed.
- Name an add-on that is not a version of StackDash, such as an API client or an MCP wrapper,
  "<your name> for StackDash".
- Write about StackDash, link to it, and use the name in articles, talks, and tutorials.

## What needs a different name

- A modified version you distribute or run for people outside your organization: give it its own
  name and logo. Keep the license and copyright notices as the AGPL requires.
- A hosted service that offers StackDash to people outside your organization: give the service and
  the application their own name and logo. That makes it a modified version, so the AGPL requires
  you to offer your users its source code. You can say it is built on StackDash.
- Other product names, domains, and social accounts that include "StackDash", or a name or logo
  close enough to be mistaken for it.
